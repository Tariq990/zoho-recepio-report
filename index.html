01 / Executive overview

One controlled system. An auditable history.

Zoho Recepio is an operator-directed outreach and relationship workflow built around Zoho Mail, an Organization → Contacts → Events ledger, evidence-based qualification, stage-specific commercial reviews, and verified message delivery. It is not simply a template generator or bulk-mail script.

Purpose

Research → decision → action

Each proposed outbound action is evaluated against company evidence, contact validity, live mailbox state, policy and prior interactions before execution.

Demonstrated application

Recepio.io domain outreach

The latest field campaign concerned a domain, but that campaign is one use case of the underlying workflow. Other purposes require updated criteria and approvals.

How to read the evidenceEvent totals show tracked interactions, not signed contracts, revenue, guaranteed deliverability, or completed commercial conversions. Historical and live-verified records are distinguished.

02 / Purpose & scope

A configurable outreach operating model.

Conventional prospecting often separates discovery, validation, copy, sending, follow-up and customer notes. Zoho Recepio brings them into one rule-governed process. The most recent campaign’s objective determined its buyer criteria and copy; it does not define the limits of the technology.

Business development

Discover organizations, confirm commercial relevance, reach attributable decision-influencing contacts and document outcomes.

Partnership outreach

Identify complementary businesses, partner programs and verifiable commercial channels before sending proposals.

Service prospecting

Use first-party evidence and validated contacts to generate relevant, source-supported B2B introductions.

Relationship follow-up

Track context, due dates, referrals, refusals and substantive replies without losing the thread or repeating actions.

Scope boundaryThese are applications the architecture can be adapted to support; this report does not claim verified deployments or outcomes for each of them.

05 / Company selection

Selection by evidence, not keywords.

A company mentioning “AI” or “front desk” is not automatically qualified. The live campaign favored companies selling receptionist or inbound-call services as a real commercial offering, supported by pricing or other attributable operating evidence.

P1

High-priority candidates

Score ≥75 with strong verified commercial capacity and contact-access requirements, plus strategic qualification.

STRONG P2

Eligible alternatives

Score ≥60, minimum commercial-capacity evidence, and a credible qualifying route.

WATCH

Not actionable

Relevant category alone cannot compensate for missing contact, capability or commercial proof.

Decisions grounded in specific research

Imagicle

A distinct enterprise AI Virtual Receptionist product, separately marketed with a named business-development route.

Neria.ai

Paid AI receptionist plans and an official partner program. The system upgraded to the commercial contact under the existing Organization.

Ruxen AI

Initially ambiguous fit resolved with first-party evidence of a separately priced AI-first answering service; qualification was recalculated.

ClearLit AI

Strong category alignment but no acceptable exact-mailbox verdict; remained blocked rather than filling a batch.

06 / Verification and safeguards

Not every published address is sendable.

Attribution and deliverability are different requirements. An MX record, guessed email pattern or generic role title does not substitute for a strict exact-mailbox result.

ControlWhat it prevents
Organization deduplicationA second company record when a newly discovered email should simply be a new Contact.
Contact provenanceInvented names, unsupported titles, guessed addresses and unjustified buyer authority.
Mailbox verdictSending to invalid, unknown or catch-all addresses without required confirmation.
Zoho history checkUnintentionally emailing someone already contacted or currently engaged.
Batch gateStarting a first-touch batch before all required approved members are prepared.
Fresh exact-message reviewSending a changed or stale draft after authorization or prior context has shifted.
Evidence of restraintWhen Hunter and ZeroBounce credits were exhausted, an address still needed a compliant strict-valid result. Unknown and catch-all verdicts were blocked, even when the company itself was attractive.

09 / Follow-up and inbound routing

Cadence and consent are decisions.

EventGoverned behavior
First touchOnly after evidence, mailbox validation, deduplication, review, authorization and batch readiness.
FU1 dueAfter three recipient-market business days, counted from an eligible prior send and within the same thread.
After FU1No second cold follow-up to that Contact. Escalation to a separate verified decision-making Contact is narrowly controlled; otherwise conclude.
Human replyLeave cold ranking; inspect the live thread, update Deal Memory and respond only to the justified next stage.
Automated replyClassify as automated, not human interest. No reflexive response.
RefusalPreserve opt-out/explicit rejection and stop outreach; no workaround via another email.
BounceQuarantine the failed mailbox; recovery requires a separate, attributed, verified contact and a new review.
Practical proof · 19 October21 FU1 follow-ups were verified, followed by a full ten-company NEW batch. The getVIA and Ruxen transport ambiguities were reconciled from existing Zoho evidence without sending duplicates.

13 / Safety, delivery and engineering

Designed to fail closed.

Mail authentication

SPF, DKIM, DMARC and alignment are monitored. A 34-of-35 aligned-pass snapshot was recorded; this does not prove primary-inbox placement.

Pacing and volume

Minimum 30-second gap, daily/hourly limits, and a HOLD on increasing send volume without sufficient clean history.

Strict deliverability checks

MX and exact-mailbox validation; invalid, unknown and catch-all results block execution unless expressly resolved under policy.

Retry-safe transport

Bounded retries for permitted read operations. Write ambiguity is reconciled through live Zoho evidence rather than automatically repeating the mutation.

Durable data integrity

Append-only events, SHA conflict checks, locks, atomic writes and Organization-level deduplication.

Testing and Git

Node.js tests, invariants and Git versioning improve traceability and regression detection; they are not an independent security certification.

14 / Operating limits

Strength includes knowing when to stop.

Observed boundaryImplication
Demonstrated objectiveDetailed operational evidence comes from a domain outreach campaign; a different offer needs revised sourcing and policy.
Provider availabilityVerification credits can limit the number of sendable new contacts even if suitable companies are discovered.
Inbox visibilitySuccessful authentication and Zoho send acceptance cannot guarantee delivery to the primary inbox.
Volume rampHigher targets can remain blocked until clean-day, bounce and placement requirements are met.
Human authorizationThe current system is operator-directed, not independently running a 24/7 unattended service.
Reply interpretationRefusals, referrals and information requests are not all campaign conversions.
External dependenciesZoho credentials, permissions, provider credits and current source evidence must be maintained.
Adapting the workflowA new use case calls for a new ICP, defensible sourcing, approved messages, fit criteria, configured sender identity, tested deliverability and accountable authorization.

Conclusion

Zoho Recepio demonstrates a controlled research-to-outreach loop: source-first selection, attributable and validated contacts, individualized messaging, governed sending, measured follow-ups and durable conversation memory. Its strongest demonstrated capability is traceability and disciplined decisions, not unverified conversion figures or autonomy claims.

16 / Glossary

Definitions for an accurate reading.

Organization

A unique company record containing any number of attributable contacts.

Contact

An identified person or official mailbox associated with the Organization.

Event

An outbound, inbound or delivery-related record; many events may belong to the same company.

NEW / FU1

Approved new first touch / eligible first follow-up after recipient-market business-day cadence.

Failover

A separately validated alternative route after documented delivery failure, never a repeat to the failed address.

P1 / P2 / WATCH

Buyer Selection priority tiers; WATCH does not authorize outreach without further evidence.

Round-trip

Comparing the Zoho-delivered MIME and message ID with the exact authorized outbound content.

Engaged

Human interaction managed by Deal Memory; not necessarily a deal or conversion.
